Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Ghostverse ("we", "us") is a ghost-hunting game at playghostverse.com. This page explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Contact: hello@playghostverse.com.

What stays on your phone

Your camera view, motion sensors and exact GPS location are used only inside your browser to run the game. Camera images and precise location are never sent to us.

What we store

  • Your game profile: hunter name, progress, coins and items. Stored in your browser and, so you don't lose it, in our database (Supabase). We create an anonymous account for you; we do not ask for your email.
  • Catches: when you catch a ghost we record the ghost, the time, your hunter name and an approximate city taken from your device's time zone (or the portal city). This powers the public live feed and leaderboard.
  • Invites: if you join through a friend's link, we record which hunter name invited you so we can give you both rewards.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Stripe tells us which item was bought so we can deliver it to your game.

Cookies, analytics and ads

  • Analytics (PostHog): anonymous play statistics, only if you tap "Accept".
  • Advertising (Google AdSense): we show ads on menu screens and optional "watch a video" rewards. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads. If you choose "No thanks", only non-personalised ads are shown. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Premium players see no ads.
  • Maps and street photos: map tiles come from MapTiler and street photos from Mapillary. These services receive standard technical data (like your IP address) when images load.

You can change your cookie choice any time: in the game, tap your name (top right) → Cookie settings.

Amazon Associates

Ghostverse is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC / Amazon EU Associates Programme, an affiliate advertising programme designed to provide a means for sites to earn fees by linking to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

Children

Ghostverse is a family-friendly game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the anonymous game data above, and we never ask for names, emails or photos. Purchases should be made by, or with permission from, a parent or guardian.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your game data at any time by emailing us with your hunter name. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to access or correct your data and to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
